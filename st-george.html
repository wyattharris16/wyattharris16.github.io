Market Pulse

St. George's median sale price was $500,000 in October 2026, and homes sold in a median of 36 days, down from 54 in September. Even so, the market still favors buyers.

Inventory is the big story. Listings topped 1,000 in September, and 56 of October's closings took a price change first.

Prices are still well below the 2022 peak. The metro's median asking price was $600,000 in October 2026, and price per square foot sat at $284, about 12% below its April 2022 high while the median price was 24% below.

For rentals, the St. George metro had a median asking rent of $2,150 in July 2026, a gross rental yield of 5.83%.

Demand drivers
  • Retirees and remote workers moving from California and Nevada
  • Tourism from Zion National Park and the surrounding red rock country
  • Utah Tech University and a growing healthcare sector
Strategies to consider
  • Buying with negotiating power while inventory is high
  • Short-term vacation rentals, only in zones where they are allowed
  • Long-term rentals in nearby Hurricane and Washington City
Risks to watch
  • Prices have not recovered to the 2022 peak, so resale timing matters
  • Short-term rental rules vary by neighborhood and city
  • Summer heat and water supply questions affect long-term growth
