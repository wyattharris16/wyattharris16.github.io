My Story

I'm a Pre-Business student at Brigham Young University's Marriott School with a real estate emphasis, and I plan to apply to the Finance major. Real estate grabbed me because it's one of the few investments you can walk through, touch and improve yourself.

My path so far has covered a lot of the real estate world. As a valuations analyst at Anchor Appraisals I produce 25+ residential valuation reports a week, which taught me how much the right comparable sales matter. At Ladera Ventures, a private equity real estate firm, I evaluated highest and best use on land holdings. At Onarock Investments I saw the capital-raising side, and now at Silver Eagle Properties I support investment and asset management for a medical office portfolio.

Before college I spent two years as a volunteer representative in Cape Verde, Africa, where I directed 180+ volunteers and became proficient in Portuguese. That experience taught me to lead people, work hard and adapt fast.

Why Utah?

Growth

Utah County alone is projected to roughly double to 1.5 million people by 2065. Growing populations need places to live.

Jobs

From Silicon Slopes in Lehi to Hill Air Force Base near Ogden, Utah has a diverse employment base along the Wasatch Front.

Home

I live and work here. Knowing the neighborhoods firsthand from appraisal work is a real edge when evaluating deals.

How I Think About Deals

  1. Start with the market. Job growth, population and supply decide whether rents can grow.
  2. Rebuild the numbers. I never trust a seller's pro forma. I underwrite my own NOI from real comps.
  3. Respect the debt. At today's rates the financing can make or break a deal.
  4. Find the value-add. The best returns come from improving a property, not just holding it.
