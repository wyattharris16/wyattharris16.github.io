Market Pulse

Ogden's median sale price was $370,163 in October 2026, with closings up 14% to 98 from 86 in September.

Buyers have the edge. Active listings reached 433, up from 249 a year earlier, and a typical home took 41 days to sell. Homes still closed at 98.6% of list price.

Zillow put Ogden's typical home value at $395,517 in August 2026, down 0.3% over the past year. Rents have been the bigger story: among the Utah cities Zillow tracks, Ogden had the largest rent increase since 2015, at about 117%.

For rentals, the Ogden metro had a median asking rent of $2,345 in July 2026, a gross rental yield of 5.81%.

Demand drivers
  • Hill Air Force Base, McKay-Dee Hospital and Weber State University
  • Lowest entry prices of the big Wasatch Front cities
  • Downtown revival around Historic 25th Street and outdoor recreation access
Strategies to consider
  • Cash-flow-focused long-term rentals
  • Older duplexes and triplexes in central Ogden
  • BRRRR (buy, rehab, rent, refinance, repeat) on homes that need work
Risks to watch
  • Older housing stock needs bigger repair budgets
  • Neighborhood quality varies block by block
  • Rising inventory may keep prices flat in the short term
