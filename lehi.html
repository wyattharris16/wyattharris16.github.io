Market Pulse

Lehi's median sale price was $589,000 in October 2026, down from $599,426 in September. Only 61 homes closed, well below the 100 in September and 122 a year earlier, so October's median should be read with some caution.

Sellers are negotiating: 36 of October's 61 sellers cut their price before closing. Entry-level homes still sell, though. Gardner Point posted 4 sales at a median of $394,950.

In September, homes sold in a median of 23 days and buyers paid about 98.5% of list price across 358 active listings, so the market still leans toward buyers.

For rentals, the Provo-Orem-Lehi metro had a median asking rent of $2,300 in July 2026, a gross rental yield of 5.46%.

Demand drivers
  • Silicon Slopes tech employers bring high-income renters and buyers
  • Newer master-planned communities like Traverse Mountain and Holbrook
  • Easy access to both Salt Lake City and Provo along I-15 and FrontRunner
Strategies to consider
  • Townhomes for young tech workers who rent before they buy
  • New-construction homes with builder incentives during slow months
  • Basement accessory dwelling units in single-family homes
Risks to watch
  • Tech layoffs can hit local demand quickly
  • Lots of new supply competes with older rentals
  • HOA rental restrictions are common in newer communities
