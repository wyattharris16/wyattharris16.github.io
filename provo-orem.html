Market Pulse

Provo's median sale price jumped to $542,500 in October 2026, up from $484,100 in September, though the average sale price ran even higher because 14 homes sold above $700,000. Monthly medians in a city this size swing a lot, so I watch the trend across several months rather than any single report.

The market leans slightly toward buyers. October ended with about 4.5 months of supply and a 34-day median time to sell, yet sellers still got 98.6% of list price, so this is not a deep-discount market.

Next door, Orem's 84057 ZIP code posted a $520,000 median single-family price and a $360,000 median condo price in the second quarter of 2026, which makes Orem condos one of the lower-cost entry points in the valley.

For rentals, the Provo-Orem-Lehi metro had a median asking rent of $2,300 in July 2026, a gross rental yield of 5.46%.

Demand drivers
  • Two large universities (BYU and Utah Valley University) create steady, year-round rental demand
  • Utah County is projected to roughly double to 1.5 million residents between 2025 and 2065
  • Close to Silicon Slopes employers in Lehi and American Fork
Strategies to consider
  • Student rentals and contracted BYU housing near campus
  • Duplexes and homes with legal basement apartments (accessory dwelling units)
  • Orem condos and townhomes as lower-cost entry points
Risks to watch
  • Provo rental licensing and occupancy rules limit how many unrelated tenants can share a unit
  • Student rentals turn over every year, which means more vacancy and wear
  • Thin cap rates: rents have not kept pace with prices
