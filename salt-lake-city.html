Market Pulse

Salt Lake City's median sale price was $590,000 in October 2026, up from $540,000 in September. A mix of higher-priced closings lifted that number, since the median list price was only $534,950.

Homes are taking longer to sell: the typical home needed 34 days in October, up from 19 in September and 20 a year earlier. Across the metro, Zillow estimated October home sales were down 22.5% from a year earlier, the steepest drop of any major metro in its report.

Countywide, the median single-family price in Salt Lake County reached a record $645,000 in the second quarter of 2026.

For rentals, the Salt Lake City-Murray metro had a median asking rent of $2,545 in July 2026, a gross rental yield of 5.54%.

Demand drivers
  • Largest job base in the state, including downtown, the University of Utah and the airport
  • Highest median asking rent of Utah's major metros
  • TRAX light rail and downtown redevelopment support walkable neighborhoods
Strategies to consider
  • Small multifamily (duplexes to fourplexes) on the west side and in Sugar House
  • Value-add renovations on older homes
  • Medium-term furnished rentals for traveling nurses and corporate stays
Risks to watch
  • Highest entry prices along the Wasatch Front
  • A falling sales volume can signal softer prices ahead
  • Older housing stock often needs bigger capital expenses
