The big idea you'll practice: NOI (net operating income) and the cap rate, the two numbers that drive how investors value income property.

Formula Cheat Sheet

  • Gross Potential Rent = monthly rent × units × 12
  • Effective Gross Income = Gross Potential Rent − vacancy
  • NOI = Effective Gross Income − operating expenses
  • Operating expenses include taxes, insurance, repairs, management, utilities the owner pays and reserves.
  • NOT in NOI: mortgage payments, interest, depreciation, income taxes.
  • Cap Rate = NOI ÷ Value
  • Value = NOI ÷ Cap Rate
